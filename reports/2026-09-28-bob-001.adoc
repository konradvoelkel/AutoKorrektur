=== Step 2: Reading the English privacy policy – control, permissions, processing

Scan::

2. App permissions
3. Processing of your photos
4. Disclosure to third parties
5. Optional diagnostics (on the device only, opt-in)
6. Contact and open source

Section 1 states:
- Using the app requires "no registration", "no user account" and "no entry of personal data".
- The app has "no internet permission". It cannot send or receive anything – neither your photos nor usage data.
- We use "no advertising SDKs", "no tracking tools" and "no analytics services".

Section 2 (App permissions) lists:

- Photos/media library: access only to the exact photo you select; results saved as JPEG to Pictures folder.

Section 3 (Processing of your photos):
- Vehicle detection (YOLOv11) and AI inpainting (MI-GAN) run "entirely on your device's processor". The models ship inside the app.
- Your photos never leave your device. There is no server they could be sent to (see §1: no internet permission).
- Intermediate results live only in memory; the only thing written to storage is what you save or share yourself.

Section 4 (Disclosure to third parties):
- Your data is neither sold nor passed on to advertising networks or any other third party.
- When you share a finished before/after image via Instagram or another app, this happens solely through Android's share sheet (Intent.ACTION_SEND): you choose the target app, and only that app receives the image.

Section 5 (Optional diagnostics):
Records: compute time of each processing step, image size in pixels, the number of detected vehicles, success or the kind of error, and once per session the app version, Android version, device manufacturer and model, memory size and processor cores. Plus a random installation ID.
Does not record: no images, no file names, no location, no contacts, no advertising ID, no account or contact details, no free text from error messages.
Where the data lives: only in a file in the app's private storage area on your device. The app does "not" transmit it – without an internet permission it could not even if it wanted to.
Export and deletion: you can pass the file on yourself at any time through the Android share sheet (for example by e-mail to the developers) or delete it with one tap. If you switch the feature off, nothing more is written.
Also mentions an independent technical log kept in the app's own storage area; it contains no images and likewise leaves the device only if you export it yourself.

Section 6 (Contact and open source):
States: "The controller responsible for data processing is Konrad Völkel, Düsseldorf; the full postal address is in the Impressum (German). For privacy questions, reach us by e-mail at kontakt [at] autokorrektur [dot] org or through the project's GitHub repository. You have the rights under Articles 15–21 GDPR and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority."

At the bottom, a note in smaller text: "Applies to the Android app; the German Datenschutzerklärung is the binding text. Hosting of this website: Impressum (German). Text as of app version v2.0.0-12-geed8819."
