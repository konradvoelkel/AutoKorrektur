Reports analyzed:: `2026-09-28-alice-001.adoc`, `2026-09-28-bob-001.adoc`, `2026-09-28-carol-001.adoc`, `2026-09-28-dave-001.adoc`, `2026-09-28-erin-001.adoc`

== Ranked improvements

=== 1. The result screen leads with the mask preview, not the result

Severity:: High
Problem:: After a photo is processed, the first screenful is a large red-overlay image captioned "Erkannte Fahrzeuge (Masken-Vorschau)". The before/after comparison — the actual product — sits below the fold and needs a scroll.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-dave-001.adoc` (Dave treated the red mask image as the finished result and went straight to sharing). Re-verified directly on a 720x1280 screen: the mask preview fills the viewport and the before/after slider only appears after one swipe.
Recommendation:: Put the before/after comparison first, immediately under the app bar. Demote the mask preview to a collapsed or optional view ("Was wurde erkannt?"). A red-splattered photo is diagnostic output and reads as an error to a novice.

=== 2. Zero detections produce no message at all

Severity:: High
Problem:: When the segmenter finds no vehicles, the app shows the unchanged photo under the caption "Erkannte Fahrzeuge (Masken-Vorschau)" and an identical before/after pair. No toast, no empty state, no explanation. The only on-screen text is the caption, which actively asserts the opposite of what happened.
Evidence:: Found during verification: processing a car-free image produced exactly two text nodes on screen ("AutoKorrektur", "Erkannte Fahrzeuge (Masken-Vorschau)") and no message of any kind.
Recommendation:: When the mask is empty, say so ("Keine Fahrzeuge erkannt — versuch ein Foto, auf dem die Autos gut sichtbar sind") and suppress or disable the export actions. A novice who photographs the wrong angle currently concludes the app is broken.

=== 3. The website still says nobody has run the app on a real phone

Severity:: High
Problem:: Both language versions of the landing page carry "Dies ist ein früher Testbuild: gerechnet wurde bisher nur im Emulator, auf echten Telefonen hat die App noch niemand laufen lassen." / "nobody has run the app on a real phone yet". `TODO.md` records the first arm64 hardware run on 2026-09-28, which crashed on the first menu item opened. The public claim is no longer true.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-carol-001.adoc` — Carol quoted that sentence as a *reason to trust the project* ("refreshingly honest") and drafted an article stating the build "has only been verified on Android emulators, not on real phones yet". A journalist persona was ready to publish a false claim. Cross-checked against commit `97af409`.
Recommendation:: Update the disclaimer in `site/index.html` and `site/en.html` to state that the first hardware run has happened, what it broke, and that the published build contains the fix; then rebuild and deploy. This is the one finding in the set where the site misleads in the *reassuring* direction.

=== 4. The risk warning is 4.8 screens below the download button

Severity:: High
Problem:: On a 375x812 phone the page is 6 screens tall. The download button is above the fold; the install instructions start 3.6 screens down; the "früher Testbuild" warning about possible crashes sits 4.8 screens down. The person most likely to tap download — a rushed non-technical visitor on a phone — will never reach it.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-alice-001.adoc` (Alice tapped download without ever encountering the maturity warning). Positions measured directly in the live page at a 375x812 viewport.
Recommendation:: Put one short line directly beneath the download button: "Früher Testbuild — Abstürze sind möglich." Keep the long version where it is. Informed consent has to sit next to the action, not five screens past it.

=== 5. "Für Instagram exportieren" opens a generic share sheet

Severity:: Medium
Problem:: The only export-looking control on the result screen is labelled "Für Instagram exportieren". Tapping it opens Android's ordinary share sheet titled "Sharing image". There is no plainly-labelled "Teilen" / "Share" button anywhere.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-dave-001.adoc` (Dave, who wanted to send to a WhatsApp group, had to guess that the Instagram button was the way to share at all). Re-verified by screenshot: the sheet offers Nearby Share, Print, Drive, Maps, Messages — nothing Instagram-specific.
Recommendation:: Rename the control to "Teilen" (or "Bild teilen"). If an Instagram-shaped export layout exists, offer it as a format choice inside the flow rather than as the name of the share action. The website promises sharing to "Instagram, Signal, Mail, was du willst" — the button should match that promise.

=== 6. Export and download are offered before there is anything to export

Severity:: Medium
Problem:: On first launch, with no photo loaded, the home screen already shows "Herunterladen" and "Für Instagram exportieren" styled as filled primary buttons — visually the most prominent things on the screen, above the two actions that actually start the flow.
Evidence:: Observed on the first-run screen in both German and English during verification.
Recommendation:: Hide or disable both until a result exists, and let "Foto aufnehmen" / "Bild auswählen" carry the primary styling on an empty screen. Primary emphasis should mark the next useful step.

=== 7. The binding legal text and the Impressum are German-only

Severity:: Medium
Problem:: The site offers a complete English path, but the English privacy policy declares the German version binding, and both English pages send the reader to a German-only Impressum for contact details.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-bob-001.adoc` (Bob could not put his name to a recommendation resting on a legal text he cannot read, and flagged it as the blocker for his data-protection officer); `2026-09-28-carol-001.adoc` (reached the Impressum for contact details and got through it only on school German).
Recommendation:: Translate the Impressum, or at minimum print the responsible person and `kontakt@autokorrektur.org` directly on the English page so contact never requires reading German. The binding-language clause can stay; the contact details should not be behind it.

=== 8. The route to the install instructions is labelled for experts

Severity:: Medium
Problem:: The secondary button reads "Installieren & prüfen" with the subtitle "Sideloading, Prüfsumme, Zertifikat". It is the signposted path for someone who has never installed an APK, but it is named in three terms that such a person does not know — "Sideloading" in particular reads as a workaround rather than the normal route.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-alice-001.adoc` — Alice saw the button, read the subtitle as technical, and dismissed it. (Note: the button is *above* the fold; the report's framing of the help as hidden was corrected on re-measurement. The problem is the label, not the placement.)
Recommendation:: Relabel to plain language, e.g. "Schritt für Schritt installieren", and keep "Prüfsumme und Zertifikat prüfen" as a secondary line inside the section for the users who want it.

=== 9. Verification instructions assume tools they do not supply

Severity:: Low
Problem:: The page states the SHA-256 is in `SHA256SUMS` and that the certificate can be checked "mit apksigner verify --print-certs", but never says where `apksigner` comes from or how to compute a checksum on either a phone or a desktop.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-carol-001.adoc` — Carol, comfortable in a terminal, could do the checksum but abandoned the certificate check as needing separate research.
Recommendation:: Add the two concrete commands (`sha256sum <file>`, and that `apksigner` ships with the Android SDK build-tools). Two lines converts a gesture at verifiability into something a reader can actually perform.

=== 10. "In zwei Tipps" overstates the flow

Severity:: Low
Problem:: The landing page promises a before/after image "zum Teilen in zwei Tipps". The observed path from launch to share sheet was four taps plus a scroll, and that is on a pre-picked photo.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-alice-001.adoc` (noted the promise), `2026-09-28-dave-001.adoc` (four taps plus scrolling to reach the share sheet).
Recommendation:: Either say "in wenigen Tipps", or make it true by fixing item 1 — with the result on top and a clearly-labelled share button, the claim is nearly accurate.

=== 11. `/download/` returns 404

Severity:: Low
Problem:: `https://autokorrektur.org/download/` serves a 404, though `/download/SHA256SUMS` and the APK itself resolve. Nothing links to the bare directory, so only a user who edits the URL by hand will meet it.
Evidence:: `2026-09-28-carol-001.adoc` step 6 (Carol did exactly that, looking for a `VERIFY.txt`); confirmed with a direct request.
Recommendation:: Low priority. If it is cheap, serve a minimal index there listing the APK and the checksum file; a verification-minded visitor is precisely the person who trims that URL.

== Findings raised but discarded

These appeared in a report and were rejected on verification. They are recorded so the same
false leads are not re-investigated next run.

- *"An undismissable Laberampel dialog blocks the photo picker" (raised Critical, dave run 1).* The emulator's camera roll holds screenshots from a sibling project. Those were image thumbnails inside the picker, not a dialog. The picker works normally.
- *"The app is in English on a German phone" (raised High, dave run 1).* A test-harness error: the device's effective locale was `en-US` and a per-app override was left in place. German was verified complete afterwards, including "Erkannte Fahrzeuge (Masken-Vorschau)" and the *VORHER* / *NACHHER* labels drawn into the exported bitmaps — the localization bug recorded in `CLAUDE.md` has not regressed.
- *"WhatsApp missing from the share sheet" (raised Critical, dave run 2).* Neither WhatsApp nor Instagram is installed on the emulator. The share sheet itself behaved correctly.
- *"Processing takes about 2 seconds" (dave run 2).* Dave was reading the mask preview as the finished result. Processing is genuinely fast on this image, but the figure should not be quoted.
- *Share-sheet chrome in English while the app is German.* Android's share UI follows the system locale, not the app's per-app language. Not an app defect.

Dave's first run was voided entirely and re-run against a cleaned, seeded environment; only the
re-run is reported in `2026-09-28-dave-001.adoc`.

== Notes

- *Cross-persona agreement.* Only one theme recurred across personas: the site addresses a
  technically confident reader, while the people it is written for (Alice, Dave) are not that reader.
  Items 4, 5 and 8 are all instances of it.
- *Worth preserving — do not "fix" these.*
  * Diagnostics is genuinely off by default, and the in-app wording matches the website's promise closely enough that Erin could confirm the claim in two taps (`2026-09-28-erin-001.adoc`).
  * "About & Licenses" names AGPLv3 and credits both bachelor theses and the browser original. It did not crash in this build — the `a7f59c5` fix holds.
  * The checksum file is well-formed and its filename matches the download link exactly; Carol called this out unprompted.
  * The landing page's one-paragraph privacy summary does not contradict the full policy anywhere — Bob went looking for a discrepancy and found none.
  * The before/after slider and the inpainting quality itself both landed well with the novice persona.
- *Isolation.* The two app personas ran sequentially on one emulator with `pm clear` between them and
  a per-app language override per persona. The device is shared with other projects, which is what
  contaminated the first run; a dedicated AVD would make future runs cheaper to trust.
