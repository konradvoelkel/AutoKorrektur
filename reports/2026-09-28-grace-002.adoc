== Context

Tool: AutoKorrektur, package `de.konradvoelkel.android.autokorrektur`. No version string or flavor
badge was visible anywhere on screen during this run, so the flavor can't be confirmed from the UI
alone.

Goal (from scenario): Grace wants to turn a photo of her parked-up street into a picture without
the cars and send it to her residents' WhatsApp group.

How run: Launched via `monkey -p de.konradvoelkel.android.autokorrektur -c
android.intent.category.LAUNCHER 1` on a freshly-data-cleared install. Two seeded photos in
DCIM/Camera: `IMG_20260927_083104.jpg` (cobblestone street, one parked blue car) and
`IMG_20260927_084512.png` (a plain building/road, no cars).

== Walkthrough

Each step follows the think-aloud loop: scan → expect → act → observe → judge.

=== Step 1: First launch

Scan:: Title "AutoKorrektur" with a three-dot overflow menu. Two large filled buttons in the
app's warm salmon/orange: "📷 Foto aufnehmen" (take photo) and "🖼 Bild auswählen" (select
image). Below them, two visibly greyed-out pill buttons: "Herunterladen" (download) and "Bild
teilen" (share image). Screenshot grace-01.png.

Expect:: The two colourful buttons are obviously "the thing to press." The grey ones look
disabled/inactive — Grace reads them as "not yet," not as broken.

Act:: Tap "Bild auswählen".

Observe:: The Android system photo picker opens directly — no in-app permission dialog first, just
the OS's own "This app can only access the photos you select" screen with Photos/Albums tabs and a
Recent grid. Screenshot grace-02.png.

Verdict:: EXPECTED — no severity issue.

Note:: On first launch, the two buttons that "look like the main thing to press" are unambiguously
"Foto aufnehmen" and "Bild auswählen" — filled, high-contrast, top of screen. The disabled
"Herunterladen"/"Bild teilen" pair reads as inactive rather than broken, which is exactly what a
first-time, nothing-loaded-yet screen should communicate.

=== Step 2: Picking the street photo

Scan:: System picker grid shows three recent images: the app's own icon tile, a building photo,
and a street photo with a visible blue parked car.

Expect:: Tapping the photo with the car will bring it into the app to be processed.

Act:: Tap the street/car thumbnail.

Observe:: Back in the app instantly: the chosen photo appears full-width, captioned "Original"
underneath, and — with no extra tap required — a line of text appears below it: "Erkenne
Fahrzeuge (50%)" (Detecting vehicles, 50%). Screenshot grace-03.png.

Verdict:: EXPECTED, and a small delight — severity n/a (positive).

Note:: Processing started automatically the moment a photo was chosen; Grace never had to find or
press a separate "start" button. The progress label uses a plain German verb phrase ("Erkenne
Fahrzeuge") rather than a technical term like "Segmentierung."

=== Step 3: Processing finishes

Scan:: (After ~1-2s more) The screen now shows a full-width split image with a centred round
drag-handle (◀▶) in the middle, and each half is captioned in a dark overlay pill: "VORHER"
(before, left) and "NACHHER" (after, right). The "after" half shows the same street with the car
gone — the road surface is filled in, though the patch has a soft, slightly smeared look up close.
Screenshot grace-04.png.

Expect:: n/a (this is the observation step).

Act:: none — just scanning.

Observe:: This before/after comparison, clearly labelled in German, is the first thing on screen
without scrolling. There is no red mask overlay, no technical screen, nothing to interpret — it is
the finished car-free picture, side by side with the original, with a slider Grace could drag.

Verdict:: EXPECTED — no severity issue (this is the answer to the "what's the first thing on
screen" question, and it's the finished picture).

Note:: The inpainted patch where the car was has a visible soft/blurred texture next to the sharp
cobblestones around it. That's a picture-quality observation, not a navigation problem — Grace
would likely still recognise "the car is gone," but a close look shows the fix isn't invisible.

=== Step 4: Scrolling for more

Scan:: Scrolling down past the before/after slider reveals a second, smaller image: the original
street photo with the car area highlighted in solid red, captioned "Erkannte Fahrzeuge
(Masken-Vorschau)" (Detected vehicles (mask preview)). Below that, "Foto aufnehmen" and "Bild
auswählen" reappear, and — no longer greyed out — "Herunterladen" and "Bild teilen" are now shown
in solid brown, i.e. enabled. Screenshots grace-05.png, grace-06.png.

Expect:: Since the result already looked finished, Grace had no reason to scroll for this — she
found it only because the walkthrough required checking. She would likely never see the red mask
image on her own.

Act:: Continue reading, no tap yet.

Observe:: The mask/detection preview exists, but it now lives below the finished result and carries
an explanit caption, rather than being the first or only thing shown.

Verdict:: EXPECTED — severity Low (informational only; "Masken-Vorschau" is still a word Grace
doesn't know, but she isn't required to read or act on this panel to finish her task).

=== Step 5: Sharing the picture

Scan:: Two enabled buttons: "🖫 Herunterladen" and "⇗ Bild teilen".

Expect:: "Bild teilen" reads plainly as "share image" — Grace expects this opens the normal Android
share/send-to sheet she already knows from her gallery app.

Act:: Tap "Bild teilen".

Observe:: The standard Android share sheet opens, titled "Sharing image", with app icons for
Nearby Share, Print, Drive, Maps, Messages, etc. (WhatsApp/Instagram are not installed on this
emulator, as expected for the test setup — not a finding). Screenshot grace-07.png.

Verdict:: EXPECTED — no severity issue.

Note:: This answers the "is there a control she recognises as share this picture" question: yes,
labelled exactly "Bild teilen," and it does what the label says on the very first tap.

=== Step 6: Back to the result

Scan:: Backing out of the share sheet returns cleanly to the same result screen (mask preview
still visible, both action buttons still enabled). Screenshot grace-08.png.

Expect:: n/a.

Act:: Press system Back once.

Observe:: No crash, no lost state, no re-processing triggered.

Verdict:: EXPECTED — no severity issue.

This completes the main run: Grace picked her street photo, watched it process with visible
progress, was shown the finished before/after picture first, found a plainly-labelled share
button, and reached the Android share sheet on the first attempt.

== Second pass: the photo with no cars

=== Step 7: Selecting the photo with no cars

Scan:: Same starting screen as Step 1 (new session state, previous result cleared from view once a
new pick starts).

Expect:: Grace picks the other seeded photo — a plain building/street with no car in it — expecting
either "nothing happens" or some kind of message, since she has no mental model of what a
"detection" app does with an image that doesn't match.

Act:: Tap "Bild auswählen" → tap the building-photo thumbnail in the system picker. Screenshots
grace-09.png, grace-10.png.

Observe:: The photo loads as "Original," and a progress line appears: "Inpainting auf dem Gerät
(75%)" (Inpainting on the device, 75%). Notably, the progress label jumped straight to the
inpainting stage, not a detection stage — meaning the app appears to run its repair pass
regardless of whether anything was actually detected to repair.

Verdict:: SURPRISING (mildly) — severity Low.

Note:: This is an internal-pipeline observation, not something Grace would consciously register —
she'd just see a progress bar tick up, same as with her street photo. It's flagged here only
because the scenario asks to record exactly what the app tells her at every step.

=== Step 8: Result for the car-free photo

Scan:: A full-width split image appears with the same centre drag-handle as before — but this time
neither half carries a "VORHER"/"NACHHER" caption. Both halves show the identical building photo.
Screenshot grace-11.png (zoomed crop grace-11-crop.png confirms no text overlay is present, even
faintly).

Expect:: n/a — observation step.

Act:: none.

Observe:: Compared to Step 3 (the car photo), the equivalent slider view is missing its "VORHER"/
"NACHHER" labels this time.

Verdict:: SURPRISING — severity Low.

Note:: This is a real, screenshot-confirmed inconsistency: the same before/after slider component
is captioned in one run and uncaptioned in the other. It doesn't block Grace's understanding here,
because the very next scroll (Step 9) gives her an unambiguous text explanation — but taken alone,
an unlabelled "here are two identical photos" slider could read as "did anything happen?"

=== Step 9: The message Grace actually gets

Scan:: Scrolling down reveals a smaller image (same building photo, no highlighting this time) with
the caption directly underneath, in plain German: "Keine Fahrzeuge erkannt" (No vehicles
detected). Below that, "Herunterladen" and "Bild teilen" are visibly greyed out/disabled again, as
they were before any photo was chosen. Screenshots grace-12.png, grace-13.png.

Expect:: n/a — observation step.

Act:: none.

Observe:: The exact, verbatim on-screen text is: "Keine Fahrzeuge erkannt". No red mask, no error
icon, no dead end — just a short factual sentence in the same type style as the earlier "Erkannte
Fahrzeuge (Masken-Vorschau)" caption. Both action buttons return to their disabled/grey state.

Verdict:: EXPECTED — no severity issue (positive finding).

Note:: This directly answers the second-pass question: Grace is told, in plain German and without
any jargon, that no vehicles were found. She is not left thinking the app is broken — the message
is short, sits right under the image she just looked at, and the greyed-out download/share buttons
reinforce "there is nothing new to export" rather than inviting her to share an unchanged photo.

button Grace could identify on sight ("Bild teilen"). Second pass also succeeded in its own

Time / steps to goal:: About 6 taps from app launch to the share sheet appearing (Bild auswählen →

retries needed.

Where the persona struggled or gave up:: Nowhere in the main run — she did not struggle or give up.
