== Findings (raw)

=== Critical severity

- *WhatsApp not in share targets*. Blocks stated goal. Share sheet appears but WhatsApp missing. Device issue or share chain configuration problem. Complete blocker for intended use case.

=== High severity

- *Label "Für Instagram exportieren" is confusing*. Says Instagram but Dave needs WhatsApp. Novice user confused about button purpose. Label creates wrong expectations even though button correctly opens generic share menu.

- *No explicit "Share" button on results screen*. After processing, four buttons shown but none labeled "Share" or "Teilen". Only "Für Instagram exportieren" looks export-like. Friction: Dave must infer which button to use.

=== Medium severity

- *No progress indicator during processing*. Completed in ~2 sec (very fast). But no progress bar, percentage, or message. Slower device or larger image could feel frozen. Progress message would help.

- *Message "No recommended people to share with" may confuse*. Technically correct but looks like error. Should clarify "Swipe to see all apps" or be omitted.

=== Low severity (positive findings)

- *Before/after slider is intuitive*. Circular control with arrows for comparing images is well-designed. Novice-friendly.

- *Processing is very fast*. ~2 seconds for segmentation + inpainting on-device. Excellent. Exceeds expectations.

- *Result quality is good*. Cars removed cleanly with plausible road reconstruction. App delivers on promise visually.

- *Gallery picker permission message is clear*. "This app can only access photos you select" reassures user.
