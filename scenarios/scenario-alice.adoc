= Scenario: Alice
:persona: Alice
:tool-type: web

== Persona

Alice is a 34-year-old mobility activist in a Düsseldorf neighbourhood initiative.
Context / device: standing at a bus stop, one-handed on an Android phone (portrait, ~375x812 CSS px), German system language, mobile data.
Expertise: novice — she installs apps from the Play Store and has never sideloaded an APK.
Mindset: rushed. She has a district council meeting in 40 minutes and wants the app on her phone before it starts.

== Goal

Alice wants to get AutoKorrektur onto her own phone right now, starting from the project's website, so she can show car-free renderings of her street at tonight's meeting.

== Starting point

She opens https://autokorrektur.org/ on her phone after a fellow activist sent her the link.

== Success criteria

She has the APK file downloaded on the phone and understands what she has to do next to install it — or she has decided she cannot do it and knows why. Either outcome is recorded.
