= Scenario: Bob
:persona: Bob
:tool-type: web

== Persona

Bob is a 58-year-old urban planner in a municipal transport department.
Context / device: at his desk on a 27" monitor, Chrome maximised, English interface, corporate network.
Expertise: intermediate with web tools, expert in his own domain; he is used to reading data-protection documents and being held to them.
Mindset: cautious and institutionally skeptical. Before he recommends anything to colleagues he must be able to state, in writing, what happens to the photographs.

== Goal

Bob wants to establish whether he may use this app on photographs taken in public streets in the course of his work — specifically whether any image data leaves the device and what the app records — so he can forward a defensible one-paragraph summary to his data-protection officer.

== Starting point

He opens https://autokorrektur.org/ on the desktop, having been sent the link in a mail, and switches to English if the page is not already.

== Success criteria

Bob can point to a specific, unambiguous statement (on the site or in the linked privacy policy) that answers "do the photos leave the device, and what is recorded?", and he knows which document is legally binding.
