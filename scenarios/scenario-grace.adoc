= Scenario: Grace
:persona: Grace
:tool-type: android
:run: 002
:kind: regression

== Persona

Grace is a 27-year-old hairdresser who is annoyed that the delivery vans on her street block the pavement with her pram.
Context / device: the installed app on a 720x1280 Android 14 phone (emulator-5554), German app language via Android per-app language, fresh app data.
Expertise: novice. She edits photos with the filter slider in her gallery app and nothing more. "Maske", "Segmentierung" and "Inpainting" mean nothing to her.
Mindset: impatient and quick to conclude something is broken. She will not scroll looking for a feature she has no reason to believe exists.

== Goal

Grace wants to turn a photo of her parked-up street into a picture without the cars and send it to her residents' WhatsApp group.

== Starting point

She taps the app icon with fresh app data. Street photos are already in the phone's gallery.

== Success criteria

A before/after image has left the app through the Android share sheet — or she gave up, and the point where she did is recorded.

== Regression note

This re-walks the path that produced UX-01 (result screen led with the red mask preview),
UX-02 (zero detections produced no message), UX-03 (export buttons live before there is
anything to export) and UX-04 ("Für Instagram exportieren" was the only share control).
All four are recorded as fixed. Grace must not be told so. In addition to her own photo,
she should at some point process a photo with no cars in it — a plain building or a room —
because that is the case UX-02 covers, and record what the app tells her.
