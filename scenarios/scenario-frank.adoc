== Persona

Frank is a 61-year-old retired postman who chairs a small residents' association.
Context / device: on an Android phone in portrait (~375x812 CSS px), German system language, sitting in his kitchen on home WiFi.
Expertise: novice. He installs things from the Play Store when his daughter tells him which button to press; he has never heard the word "sideloading" and does not know what a checksum is.
Mindset: rushed but conscientious — he wants the app tonight, and he is the kind of person who would be upset to find out afterwards that he had been warned about something and missed it.

== Goal

Frank wants to get the app onto his phone from the website, and he wants to know before he starts whether this is finished software he can rely on or something that might break.

== Starting point

He opens https://autokorrektur.org/ on his phone, sent by a neighbour.

== Success criteria

The APK is downloaded and Frank can say, in his own words, (a) what he has to do next to install it and (b) how finished the software is. If he cannot say one of those, record which.

== Regression note

This scenario re-walks the path that produced UX-05 (risk warning 4.8 screens below the
download button) and UX-06 (install route labelled "Sideloading, Prüfsumme, Zertifikat").
Both are recorded as fixed and deployed. Frank must not be told they were fixed; the point
is whether a fresh novice now meets the warning and understands the install route unaided.
